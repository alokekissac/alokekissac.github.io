Privacy Policy

Resumae · last updated 1 October 2026

Resumae is a personal tool built and run by Aloke K. Issac to tailor his own CV to job descriptions. It is not a commercial service and has no users other than its owner. This policy explains what the application accesses and what happens to that data.

Who runs this

Aloke K. Issac, Dublin, Ireland. Contact: alokekissac@gmail.com. The application runs on a private server controlled solely by the owner.

What the application accesses

With the owner's explicit Google authorisation, Resumae uses the following Google APIs, acting only on the owner's own Google account:

  • Google Docs — reads one CV template document and writes tailored copies of it.
  • Google Drive — copies that template, exports the result as PDF and DOCX, and stores those files in the owner's own Drive.
  • Google Sheets — reads a queue of job descriptions the owner has entered, and writes status back to a tracking sheet.

Job descriptions are supplied by the owner, pasted from public job advertisements.

What it does not do

  • It does not read email, contacts, calendar, photos, or any Drive file outside the documents named above.
  • It does not collect data from anyone other than the owner.
  • It does not sell, rent, share, or transfer data to third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.
  • It has no analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies on this page.

Where data is stored

Generated CVs stay in the owner's own Google Drive. Application state — job rows, run history — is stored in a database on the owner's private server, hosted in Amazon Web Services' Ireland region. Finished CVs are emailed to the owner's own address and nowhere else.

Third-party processing

To write the tailored text, job-description content and the owner's career history are sent to language-model APIs (Anthropic, and OpenRouter for scoring) under those providers' own terms. Only the owner's own career information and publicly posted job descriptions are sent. No other person's data is involved.

Retention and deletion

Generated documents remain in the owner's Drive until he deletes them. Google authorisation can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's access. Deleting the server removes all application state.

Security

Access to the application is password-protected and served over HTTPS. OAuth tokens are held encrypted on the owner's server and are never shared.

Resumae is a single-user personal project. If you have reached this page from a Google consent screen, the account being asked for access is the owner's own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change with it.
